# Work Dashboard

A running system for keeping track of everything on your plate as a junior JO, without having to hold it all in your head.

## How this works

- **`LOG.md`** — the low-friction side. Just dump context in whenever: what happened today, something you were told, a task someone handed you, a qual you started, a thing you don't want to forget. No formatting required, no need to categorize it yourself. Newest entries go at the top.
- **`DASHBOARD.md`** — the organized side. Periodically (ask, or I'll offer), I'll read through `LOG.md` and fold anything new into the right section of the dashboard: quals in progress, collateral duties, deadlines, open threads, etc. This is the "what's my status right now" view.

## Workflow

1. Talk to me like you're briefing a chief of staff — "started my X qual today," "board wants Y by next Friday," "still owe the XO that memo." I'll capture it in the log.
2. When you want a clean status check, say so and I'll refresh the dashboard from the log.
3. Sections in the dashboard can change shape as your workload does — nothing here is fixed. Junior JO workload compounds fast (quals stack on watches stack on collateral duties), so the dashboard's job is to keep that stack visible, not to enforce a particular structure.
